**The detail follows you from one display to the other.**

## What's new

- **The island follows where you work.** With a display without a notch connected:
  - **On the Mac**, the island shows your usage right of the notch.
  - **On the other display**, it fades away and the menu bar there carries the detail.
  - **The centered capsule** does the mirror image.
  - **A MacBook on its own** keeps the island as before.
- **Hover opens a card.** Pointing at the notch no longer grows the island into the panel: the panel opens in a card just below the notch, rounded on all four corners, like the capsule's card on an external display.
- **Smoother.** The island's sides stretch and fold in one movement instead of appearing at once.

## Install (preview build, not notarized)

With Homebrew:

```bash
brew install --cask florentmaille/tap/notch-usage
```

Already installed? `brew upgrade --cask notch-usage`. Otherwise:

1. Download `NotchUsage-0.5.7-preview.zip` below and unzip it.
2. Move **NotchUsage.app** to your Applications folder, replacing the previous version if you have one.
3. Open it. macOS says it cannot verify the developer: click **Done**.
4. Open **System Settings → Privacy & Security**, scroll down and click **Open Anyway** next to Notch Usage, then confirm.

Upgrading: macOS asks again for Keychain access. Click **Always Allow**.

**Checksum (SHA-256)** of the zip: `fe70dd4ecbe20126fad45f9e9947e934b20e5d1dde023c901e68d22b54ecb0ba`

## Known limitations

- The capsule needs a visible menu bar: with *Automatically hide and show the menu bar*, it does not show.
- On displays narrower than 1700 pt, the capsule is not shown, so it never covers app menus; the menu bar icon covers those displays.
- Not notarized: the Keychain may ask again after you replace the app with a newer build. Signed releases will fix this.
- Uses undocumented Anthropic endpoints that may change at any time. Read the [risk note](https://github.com/florentmaille/Notch-Usage#%EF%B8%8F-risk-note-please-read).

Requires macOS 14 (Sonoma) or later, Apple Silicon or Intel.
