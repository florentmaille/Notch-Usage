**The island and the capsule stay visible on both displays, and Settings are no longer cut.**

A small release after 0.6.2: one change asked for after living with two displays, and the last findings of the audit.

## What changes

- **The island and the capsule no longer follow the display you work on.** With a display without a notch connected, the island was erased while you worked on the other display, and the capsule shrank to its ring while you worked on the Mac. Both now always show the same detail. The menu bar icon still shrinks to its ring on the Mac's menu bar, where the island carries the detail.
- **No more click watching.** With the menu bar icon set to *Never*, the app watched clicks and app switches to know which display was active. Nothing needs it any more: it is removed.

## What's fixed

- **Settings are no longer cut.** With *Advanced* expanded, the card was capped at 480 pt: the title, *Back* and the bottom were cut, on the island's card and on the capsule's. The card now takes the height of its content, up to the screen.
- **Capsule card and menus.** The card could close while you were choosing a language. It stays open, like the island's.
- **Full screen.** The hover strip at the top of the screen was wider than the island it reveals, which could make the island flicker with the pointer resting at its ends.
- **This week: "Not enough readings yet".** The message was never shown: a curve was drawn from a single reading. It now takes two readings since the reset.
- **This week: a reset at midnight** showed six days under the curve instead of seven.
- **Forecast without a reset time.** When Claude gives no reset time for the 5-hour window, a rising pace was reported as "holds until reset", which switched the critical threshold off. No forecast is shown in that case and the thresholds apply.
- **VoiceOver.** The session key field is read "Session key", and the *This week* section says whether it is collapsed or expanded.

## Under the hood

- The island, the menu bar icon and the capsules moved out of the app model into their own class. No behaviour change.

## Updating

From 0.5.8 or later, use **Settings → Check for updates**. From an older version:

```bash
brew update && brew upgrade --cask notch-usage
```

## Install (preview build, not notarized)

With Homebrew:

```bash
brew install --cask florentmaille/tap/notch-usage
```

Already installed? `brew update && brew upgrade --cask notch-usage`. Otherwise:

1. Download `NotchUsage-0.6.3-preview.zip` below and unzip it.
2. Move **NotchUsage.app** to your Applications folder, replacing the previous version if you have one.
3. Open it. macOS says it cannot verify the developer: click **Done**.
4. Open **System Settings → Privacy & Security**, scroll down and click **Open Anyway** next to Notch Usage, then confirm.

Upgrading: macOS asks again for Keychain access. Click **Always Allow**.

## Known limitations

- Escape closes a panel opened by hovering only after a click inside it; moving the pointer away still closes it.
- The capsule needs a visible menu bar: with *Automatically hide and show the menu bar*, it does not show.
- On displays narrower than 1700 pt, the capsule is not shown, so it never covers app menus; the menu bar icon covers those displays.
- Not notarized: the Keychain may ask again after you replace the app with a newer build. Signed releases will fix this.
- Uses undocumented Anthropic endpoints that may change at any time. Read the [risk note](https://github.com/florentmaille/Notch-Usage#%EF%B8%8F-risk-note-please-read).

Requires macOS 14 (Sonoma) or later, Apple Silicon or Intel.
