**A forecast for the weekly limits, and a quiet top edge in full screen.**

## What's new

- **Weekly limits get a forecast too.** The weekly limit for all models and the per-model weekly limits (Fable, for example) now show *At this pace (this week): blocked Thu 23:10* or *holds until reset*, in the panel and in the menu bar menu. The pace is the average since the week started, nights included, so a busy afternoon does not announce a block that will not come. The notch itself still follows the 5-hour window.
- **Nothing at the top of the screen in full screen.** In full-screen apps, a weekly limit above its threshold left a thin orange line at the top of the screen, sometimes for days. The notch now stays fully hidden until you point at the top of the screen; macOS notifications still warn you.
- **Aligned panel rows.** The highlighted limit in the panel no longer shifts its text inward.

## Install (preview build, not notarized)

With Homebrew:

```bash
brew install --cask florentmaille/tap/notch-usage
```

Already installed? `brew upgrade --cask notch-usage`. Otherwise:

1. Download `NotchUsage-0.5.3-preview.zip` below and unzip it.
2. Move **NotchUsage.app** to your Applications folder, replacing the previous version if you have one.
3. Open it. macOS says it cannot verify the developer: click **Done**.
4. Open **System Settings → Privacy & Security**, scroll down and click **Open Anyway** next to Notch Usage, then confirm.

Upgrading: macOS asks again for Keychain access. Click **Always Allow**.

**Checksum (SHA-256)** of the zip: `9f513f08fa256c557a44ec20dff548aa581779074187eed3f5cb542409faa8db`

## Known limitations

- Not notarized: the Keychain may ask again after you replace the app with a newer build. Signed releases will fix this.
- The weekly forecast needs half a day of the week to have passed; before that, weekly limits use thresholds.
- Uses undocumented Anthropic endpoints that may change at any time. Read the [risk note](https://github.com/florentmaille/Notch-Usage#%EF%B8%8F-risk-note-please-read).

Requires macOS 14 (Sonoma) or later, Apple Silicon or Intel.
