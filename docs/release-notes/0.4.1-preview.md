**The halo beats when a block is imminent.** A static red edge ends up part of the scenery; peripheral vision catches motion, not colour.

## What's new

- **Imminence pulse.** When the forecast puts the block under 30 minutes, the halo around the notch beats once a minute (a double heartbeat: the edge brightens and the halo widens, in about a second). Under 10 minutes, every 30 seconds. Nothing else moves: not the edge, not the notch width, not the panel.
- **Only then.** No pulse on the amber or orange edges, none once the limit is reached, none when the red comes from a 95 % threshold without a forecast, none while hovering or with the panel pinned, and none with Reduce Motion on.
- Same rhythm on the pill (displays without a notch). VoiceOver announcements unchanged.
- Same budget: the pulse rides on the existing 30-second refresh, no timer, no request.

## Install (preview build, not notarized)

With Homebrew:

```bash
brew install --cask florentmaille/tap/notch-usage
```

Already installed? `brew upgrade --cask notch-usage`. Otherwise:

1. Download `NotchUsage-0.4.1-preview.zip` below and unzip it.
2. Move **NotchUsage.app** to your Applications folder, replacing the previous version if you have one.
3. Open it. macOS says it cannot verify the developer: click **Done**.
4. Open **System Settings → Privacy & Security**, scroll down and click **Open Anyway** next to Notch Usage, then confirm.

Upgrading: macOS asks again for Keychain access. Click **Always Allow**.

## Known limitations

- Not notarized: the Keychain may ask again after you replace the app with a newer build. Signed releases will fix this.
- The forecast covers the 5-hour window only; weekly limits use thresholds.
- Uses undocumented Anthropic endpoints that may change at any time. Read the [risk note](https://github.com/florentmaille/Notch-Usage#%EF%B8%8F-risk-note-please-read).

Requires macOS 14 (Sonoma) or later, Apple Silicon or Intel.
