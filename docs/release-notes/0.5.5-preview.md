**The notch tells you when Claude Code is waiting for you.**

## What's new

- **Claude Code waits, the notch shows it.** Turn on **Follow Claude Code** in Settings. When a Claude Code session, in the terminal or in the Code tab of the Claude app, asks for an approval, asks you a question or finishes its turn, the right side of the notch shows a violet hand, a violet question bubble or a white check, and keeps it until you act. The word shows for 4 seconds when it arrives, then the glyph stays alone. Several sessions: one glyph and a count.
- **Your limit comes first.** When a block is under 30 minutes or a limit is reached, the limit keeps the right side and the waiting session becomes a violet dot on the gauge.
- **Hover to see which session.** A *Claude Code* section above the limits lists each waiting session, with its title from the Claude app or its project folder, what it waits for and since when. Click a row to bring its app to the front. Same list in the menu bar menu.
- **Optional notification.** *Notify me when Claude waits* adds a macOS notification for approvals and questions. Off by default.

## How it works, and what it touches

- Turning on **Follow Claude Code** adds seven hook entries to `~/.claude/settings.json`, all pointing at a small script in `~/Library/Application Support/NotchUsage/`. Turning it off removes exactly those entries; your own hooks are left untouched. It takes effect at once, even in sessions already open.
- The script appends each event to a file in the same folder. Notch Usage keeps only the event type, session, folder, title and tool name, never your prompts or Claude's answers, and empties the file as soon as it has read it. Nothing leaves your Mac.
- The Chat tab of the Claude app is not covered: it runs no hooks.

## Install (preview build, not notarized)

With Homebrew:

```bash
brew install --cask florentmaille/tap/notch-usage
```

Already installed? `brew upgrade --cask notch-usage`. Otherwise:

1. Download `NotchUsage-0.5.5-preview.zip` below and unzip it.
2. Move **NotchUsage.app** to your Applications folder, replacing the previous version if you have one.
3. Open it. macOS says it cannot verify the developer: click **Done**.
4. Open **System Settings → Privacy & Security**, scroll down and click **Open Anyway** next to Notch Usage, then confirm.

Upgrading: macOS asks again for Keychain access. Click **Always Allow**.

**Checksum (SHA-256)** of the zip: `1c5ad80ae6e79b7d0626ea6c5bc1cc93c47c7ea8b81db01c9f673511e57bd3b8`

## Known limitations

- Before uninstalling, turn off **Follow Claude Code**: otherwise the hook entries stay in `~/.claude/settings.json` and the script keeps writing events that nothing reads.
- Not notarized: the Keychain may ask again after you replace the app with a newer build. Signed releases will fix this.
- The weekly forecast needs half a day of the week to have passed; before that, weekly limits use thresholds.
- Uses undocumented Anthropic endpoints that may change at any time. Read the [risk note](https://github.com/florentmaille/Notch-Usage#%EF%B8%8F-risk-note-please-read).

Requires macOS 14 (Sonoma) or later, Apple Silicon or Intel.
