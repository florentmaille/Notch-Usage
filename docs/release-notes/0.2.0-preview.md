**Notch Usage now speaks French.** Plus a smoother browser sign-in.

## What's new

- **French interface.** Everything is translated: the notch, the panel, sign-in, settings, errors and VoiceOver. Durations read naturally ("dans 2 h 14 min", "il y a 12 min").
- **Language setting.** Notch Usage follows your Mac's language. To override it, open **Settings → Language** and pick **System**, **English** or **Français**, then click **Restart**.

## Fixes

- Browser sign-in no longer fails over and over with "Claude rejected the credentials" after an expired claude.ai session. Signing out now also clears the claude.ai cookies.
- The expanded panel folds away when the sign-in window opens, instead of covering it.
- The sign-in window opens in front, even over a full-screen app, and no longer stays above other apps: switch to your mail to fetch the sign-in code, then click **Sign in with browser** again to bring the window back.
- Retrying an expired Claude Code session now tells you what to do: run `claude` once in Terminal, then try again.

## Install (preview build, not notarized)

This preview is not signed with an Apple Developer ID, so macOS blocks it the first time.

1. Download `NotchUsage-0.2.0-preview.zip` below and unzip it.
2. Move **NotchUsage.app** to your Applications folder, replacing the previous version if you have one.
3. Open it. macOS says it cannot verify the developer: click **Done**.
4. Open **System Settings → Privacy & Security**, scroll down and click **Open Anyway** next to Notch Usage, then confirm.

Prefer the Terminal? This removes the download quarantine flag instead:

```bash
xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/NotchUsage.app
```

Upgrading from 0.1.0: macOS asks again for Keychain access. Click **Always Allow**.

**Checksum (SHA-256)** of the zip: `5546d660f814aa64e4296d67ab228af1720cd2e6c24399240d1c3647ed547320`

## Known limitations

- Not notarized: the Keychain may ask again after you replace the app with a newer build. Signed releases will fix this.
- Uses undocumented Anthropic endpoints that may change at any time. Read the [risk note](https://github.com/florentmaille/Notch-Usage#%EF%B8%8F-risk-note-please-read).

Requires macOS 14 (Sonoma) or later, Apple Silicon or Intel.
